= Learn about SnapLock data protection for ASA r2 storage systems 
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
ONTAP SnapLock is a high-performance compliance solution for organizations that use WORM storage to retain files in unmodified form for regulatory and governance purposes. You can run SnapLock in Compliance or Enterprise modes, which offer different levels of WORM data protection. SnapLock Compliance and Enterprise modes are not supported on primary clusters in  SAN environments, but you can enable SnapLock for SnapVault on your secondary systems to protect secondary snapshots from accidental or malicious deletion.

The SnapLock Compliance Clock must be initialized to link:link:../data-protection/snapshot-replication.html[protect snapshots on your secondary systems with SnapLock for SnapVault].

Beginning with ONTAP 9.20.1, you can configure backend SnapLock for SnapVault replication over existing FC fabrics. To do so, your ONTAP system backend must be configured for FC replication and both the FC fabric and the intercluster IP network must be operational. Replication data can transport over the FC fabric, but the intercluster IP network is required for cluster communications and replication management functions.

Learn to link:/https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/ontap/data-protection/configure-backend-fc-replication.html[configure your ONTAP system backend for FC replication^].

.Related information

== Initialize the SnapLock Compliance Clock

If you want to protect destination snapshots on a secondary system with SnapLock for SnapVault, you must first initialize the SnapLock Compliance Clock on your local and destination clusters.

.Steps

. Select *Cluster > Overview*.
. In the *Nodes* section, select *Initialize SnapLock Compliance Clock*.
. Select *Initialize*.
. Verify that the Compliance Clock is initialized.
.. Select *Cluster > Overview*.   
.. In the *Nodes* section, select image:icon_show_hide.png[three blue columns on white background]; then select *SnapLock Compliance Clock*.

.What’s next?

After you have initialized the SnapLock Compliance Clock on your local and destination clusters, you are ready to link:../data-protection/snapshot-replication.html[create a replication relationship with SnapLock for SnapVault].
